About This Space

Even when you are browsing in another tab on your Chromebook, ColorChat updates your browser tab title with an unread count and adds a bright red dot to the tab icon.

What We Offer

Tab Badge Alerts

When you switch to another tab, new messages update the tab title with a live unread counter like (1) New Message!

Red Icon Dot

The tiny icon at the top of your Chrome tab automatically switches to a bright red alert badge whenever you have unread messages.

Auto-Clear on Return

As soon as you click back onto this tab, the unread count resets and the tab icon returns to normal.
